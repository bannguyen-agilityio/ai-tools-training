# Ticket Analysis Workshop

## Ticket ID: RENT-201 — Incorrect Bill Balance

**1. What it asks:**
The ticket asks us to correct the outstanding balance of a bill by subtracting the payments applied to it from the total bill amount.

**2. What's ambiguous:**
The ticket does not clearly specify which payments are valid and which are not.

**3. First step:**
I would first verify that the bill total and the payments applied to it are correct. Then, I would subtract the payments from the total bill amount to calculate the outstanding balance and compare it with the displayed balance.

### Questions for the reporter
1. Can you give me two or three specific bills where the balance is wrong, including what’s displayed and what you expected, and how you arrived at the expected figure?
2. What do the affected bills have in common (partial payments, multiple payments, adjustments, a certain payment method, a certain time)?
3. Roughly when did you first notice this, and were these bills correct before?
4. How should payments that are reversed, refunded, or only partially applied affect the balance?
5. Is the displayed balance wrong only on screen, or also in emails, exports, or reports?
