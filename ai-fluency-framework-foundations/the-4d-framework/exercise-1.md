# [Exercise 1: Apply the 4D's](https://academy.claude.com/courses/ai-fluency-framework-foundations/the-4d-framework#exercises)

## Instructions

Apply the 4D's.

Pick one of these collaboration scenarios and consider how you might apply the 4D Framework:

### Communication project

You're working with an AI assistant to draft a series of emails for a marketing campaign.

- **Delegation:** What aspects of this project would you handle yourself vs. collaborate on with AI?
- **Description:** How would you communicate your vision for the campaign's tone, purpose, and success criteria to the AI?
- **Discernment:** What criteria would help you evaluate whether the AI-drafted emails meet your needs?
- **Diligence:** What considerations around transparency and responsibility would be important?

## Solution

### Communication project

#### Delegation

- **Handle myself**
  - Define the campaign’s goals, audience, offer, and key messages.
  - Make the final editorial decisions and approve each email.
- **Collaborate with AI**
  - Brainstorm ideas.
  - Draft the email sequence.
  - Create subject-line variations.

#### Description

- Explain the campaign’s purpose and target audience.
- Provide verified product details and examples of the brand’s voice.
- Specify a friendly, confident tone.
- Define the format: three emails, each under 150 words, with one clear call to action.
- Explain success criteria, such as relevant clicks and sign-ups.

#### Discernment

- Check that product details and claims are accurate.
- Evaluate whether the tone matches the brand and audience.
- Ensure each email has a distinct purpose and clear call to action.
- Check that subject lines accurately reflect the content.
- Request revisions for generic, repetitive, or misleading wording.

#### Diligence

- Protect customer information and confidential business data.
- Avoid unsupported claims, invented testimonials, and false urgency.
- Take responsibility for the final content and review every email before sending.
